# Mock world — notices and attributions

**SYNTHETIC DATA.** Every company, carrier, person, email address and rate in this directory is
fictional and generated by `packages/agents/src/mock-world/build.ts` (seed 20261021). Domains use the
reserved `.example` TLD (RFC 2606). Names were checked against web search only — verify company
registries before publishing them as anything other than fiction.

- **Rate levels** are seeded draws inside 2026 market bands informed by public indexes and notices
  (Drewry World Container Index 2026-10-15, Xeneta market updates, published FAK notices, a public
  forwarder general tariff). **No tariff table was copied.** See `docs/research/mock-world-spec.md`.
- **Exchange rates** (`fx.json`): Source: ECB euro foreign exchange reference rates, 21 October 2026,
  unmodified. Published by the ECB for information purposes only.
- **UN/LOCODEs** are used as factual location identifiers (© UNECE; not endorsed by UNECE).
- **Trading-condition names** (e.g. "FENEX-style", "ADSp-style") name a style only; no text from any
  association's conditions is reproduced.
